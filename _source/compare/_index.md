Cipi is **free, open source and runs on your own server**. Below, how it compares with each tool people weigh it against: what each costs, where it runs, and when the other tool is the better choice.

| You want | Look at |
| --- | --- |
| A panel for your own VPS, without the subscription | [Forge](/alternative-to-laravel-forge), [Ploi](/alternative-to-ploi), [Vito Deploy](/alternative-to-vito-deploy) |
| No servers at all | [Laravel Cloud](/alternative-to-laravel-cloud), [Render](/alternative-to-render) |
| Containers for many stacks | [Coolify](/alternative-to-coolify), [Dokku](/alternative-to-dokku) |
| Shared hosting for customers | [cPanel](/alternative-to-cpanel), [Plesk](/alternative-to-plesk) |

Every page was checked against public pricing and documentation in November 2026. Products change: check before deciding, and [tell us](https://github.com/cipi-sh/website/issues) if something is out of date.
