## Send a postcard {#send}

Running Cipi on a server? Tell us about it. The [postcards wall](/postcards) collects short messages from people who run Cipi: which provider, which region, how many apps and what they host.

It takes a minute:

1. Open [cipi.sh/postcards](/postcards#send).
2. Choose your **provider** and type the **region** (suggestions appear as you type).
3. Add your name and city if you like. Both are optional.
4. Write a few lines, up to 280 characters, without links.
5. Tick the box to agree to publication and press **Send postcard**.

<a class="btn btn-lime" href="/postcards#send">Send a postcard</a>

## What happens next {#review}

Your postcard is not published straight away. Netlify, which hosts this site, filters spam first. Then a person reads it, and only after that does it appear on the wall, usually within a few days.

Only the fields you filled in are shown. There is no email field, and links, HTML and addresses are refused, so the wall stays free of spam.

## Change or remove a postcard {#remove}

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/cipi-sh/website/issues/new) or write on [Telegram](https://t.me/cipicloud), saying which postcard is yours. It will be changed or removed.

## Why postcards {#why}

Cipi has no telemetry: it never reports how many servers run it or where. Postcards are the opposite: you choose to tell us. They help new users see that Cipi runs on real servers with their provider, and they are a nice thing to read for the people who build it.
